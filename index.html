About AMS PRIME LLC FZE

Complete Uniforms & Safety Equipment Solutions From Design to Delivery

Headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, AMS PRIME LLC FZE is an established industry leader specializing in the manufacturing, custom branding, and wholesale distribution of institutional workwear, school apparel, culinary uniforms, and certified industrial personal protective equipment (PPE).

With manufacturing roots and regional uniform supply experience across the UAE dating back over a decade (operating under the renowned AMS heritage), we understand the unique demands of Middle Eastern operational climates—delivering breathable, durable, and certified garments that stand up to daily industrial and institutional wear.
