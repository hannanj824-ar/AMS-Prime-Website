Frequently Asked Questions

What are your minimum order quantities (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by product line: custom school uniforms and corporate suits generally start from 25–50 sets, polo t-shirts from 100 pcs, and safety gear (vests, helmets, shoes) can be supplied from 20–50 units up to thousands for major construction contractors.

Can you provide physical samples before we confirm a bulk order?

Yes. We provide sample garments with your customized logo embroidery or print, along with fabric swatch cards, to ensure full satisfaction before mass production commences.

What certifications accompany your safety products?

Our safety vests carry EN ISO 20471 certification; safety footwear complies with CE EN ISO 20345 (S3/SRC rated); hard hats comply with ANSI Z89.1 / EN 397; and safety harnesses adhere to EN 361 and OSHA standards. Full certificates are supplied with quotation packs.

What does Open 24 Hours / 24/7 Availability mean?

Our quotation response line, client service team, and emergency PPE dispatch operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can call or message our WhatsApp (+971 50 353 7101) at any time.
