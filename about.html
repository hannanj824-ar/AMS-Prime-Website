About AMS PRIME LLC FZE

Based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, we are an integrated uniform manufacturer and certified industrial personal protective equipment (PPE) provider dedicated to excellence, innovation, and 24/7 service.

Our UAE Heritage

A Decade of Supplying Quality Uniforms Across the UAE

With roots supplying all types of uniforms throughout the UAE dating back over a decade (as highlighted in our historical AMS Global foundations), AMS PRIME LLC FZE has evolved into a premier full-cycle uniform manufacturing and safety solutions authority.

Our core operating philosophy is simple: "Complete Uniforms Solution: From design to delivery." We oversee each stage of the garment lifecycle—from yarn and fabric procurement to computer-aided pattern design, cutting, multi-head computerized embroidery, high-visibility tape heat-welding, and rigorous multi-stage quality control before dispatch.

"We believe that a uniform is not merely work clothing; it is the physical embodiment of your institution's prestige, safety culture, and organizational pride."
